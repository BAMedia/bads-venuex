Where to eat

Kin Khao, Union Square, SF (Parc 55 hotel, 55 Cyril Magnin St) · Thai · Michelin One Star. Michelin praises its "layered, vibrantly flavorful Thai cooking" with a produce-driven northern bent, tucked into an alcove of the hotel. +1 415-362-7456. https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/california/san-francisco/restaurant/kin-khao

Rich Table, Hayes Valley, SF (199 Gough St) · Californian · in the Michelin Guide. A "petite Hayes Valley delight" that is packed nightly; a short walk from Sunday's opera matinee. +1 415-355-9085. https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/california/san-francisco/restaurant/rich-table

Zuni Café, Market St, SF (1658 Market St) · Californian · in the Michelin Guide. Michelin calls it a San Francisco institution known for its laid-back California vibe and locally sourced food, in an iconic, art-filled space. +1 415-552-2522. https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/california/san-francisco/restaurant/zuni-cafe

La Taqueria, Mission, SF (2889 Mission St) · Mexican · in the Michelin Guide (carried). A local favorite that "always has a queue," a few blocks from Monday's procession route. (415) 285-7117. https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/california/san-francisco/restaurant/la-taqueria

Where to stay

Per SF Travel, Union Square has most major hotels and the best transit links, with easy transit to Chase Center and the opera (note the Powell BART entrance closure at 4th and Market from Monday). Hayes Valley and Civic Center put you by the opera for Sunday's matinee; Mission Bay is closest to Chase Center for both Warriors games; the Mission puts you on Monday's procession route but expect evening closures. Skip Marina/Van Ness-area hotels Sunday morning if you need to drive out before noon. https://www.sftravel.com/neighborhoods
