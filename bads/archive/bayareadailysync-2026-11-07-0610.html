Getting around

Youth 18 and under ride free (not cable cars). Chase Center tickets are a same-day Muni fare Sat and Mon. Sunday: F streetcar switchbacks toward the Ferry Building 8:30 am–3 pm and the 8, 28 and 39 reroute for the parade; the 6 Hayes/Parnassus outbound reroutes for Head West. https://www.sfmta.com/getting-around/muni/fares
