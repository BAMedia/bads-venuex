**Rich Table, Hayes Valley, SF (199 Gough St) · Californian · in the Michelin Guide.** A "petite Hayes Valley delight" that is packed nightly; near Civic Center. +1 415-355-9085. https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/california/san-francisco/restaurant/rich-table

**Zuni Café, Market St, SF (1658 Market St) · Californian · in the Michelin Guide.** Michelin calls it a San Francisco institution with a laid-back California vibe and locally sourced food, in an iconic, art-filled space. +1 415-552-2522. https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/california/san-francisco/restaurant/zuni-cafe

**La Taqueria, Mission, SF (2889 Mission St) · Mexican · in the Michelin Guide.** A local favorite that "always has a queue." (415) 285-7117. https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/california/san-francisco/restaurant/la-taqueria
