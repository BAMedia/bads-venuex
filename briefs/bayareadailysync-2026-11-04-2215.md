## What's on (each confirmed on an official page)

**1) Ferry Plaza Farmers Market (Thursday edition): Thu Nov 5, 10 am–2 pm.** Ferry Building, Embarcadero and Market. Street-food lunch and fresh produce. Free entry.
https://www.foodwise.org/farmers-markets/ferry-plaza-farmers-market/

**2) Exploratorium After Dark – Digest This: Thu Nov 5, 6–10 pm.** Pier 15. Adults only (18+); a night on the history and science of how we eat, "from the first taste to the final flush." Tickets online; free for members.
https://www.exploratorium.edu/visit/calendar/after-dark-digest-this

**3) Warriors vs. Miami Heat: Thu Nov 5, 7 pm.** Chase Center. Klay Thompson and Andrew Wiggins return with Miami. TV: NBCSBA, NBA TV. Your Chase Center event ticket doubles as a Muni fare that day (not cable cars).
https://www.nba.com/heat/game/0022600188-heat-vs-warriors-san-francisco-ca-11-05-2026

**4) SF Symphony: Isabel Leonard Sings Ravel: Fri Nov 6, 7:30 pm.** Davies Symphony Hall, 201 Van Ness Ave. Fabien Gabel conducts Ravel's *Shéhérazade*, d'Indy and Strauss. Free pre-concert talk one hour before; Día de los Muertos altars in the lobby through Nov 11.
https://www.sfsymphony.org/Buy-Tickets/2026-27/Isabel-Leonard-Sings-Ravel

**5) Every Saturday Night (A.C.T. / SFBATCO): final performances through Sat Nov 7.** Strand Theater, 1127 Market St. A new musical moving between today's Fillmore and the 1950s "Harlem of the West." Ages 13+. Showtimes on the ticket page.
https://www.act-sf.org/whats-on/activate/every-saturday-night

**6) Ferry Plaza Farmers Market (Saturday): Sat Nov 7, 8 am–2 pm.** Ferry Building, Embarcadero and Market. The big weekly edition of the market; free entry. Sunny, near 73.
https://www.foodwise.org/farmers-markets/ferry-plaza-farmers-market/

**7) Veterans Day Parade: Sun Nov 8, 11 am.** Fisherman's Wharf. Formation at 10 am on North Point (Powell to the Embarcadero); the route runs up the Embarcadero north of North Point and along Jefferson to Aquatic Park. Free to watch. The F streetcar turns back at the Ferry Building 8:30 am–3 pm.
https://www.sfmta.com/travel-updates/veterans-day-parade-sunday-november-8-2026

Also Sunday: Head West Marketplace in Hayes Valley, with Hayes, Linden and Octavia closed around Hayes and Octavia 6 am–7 pm (event hours aren't posted on the organizer's site). https://www.sfmta.com/travel-updates/head-west-marketplace-sunday-november-8-2026

## Where to eat
No Yelp ratings (Yelp blocks automated loads). New picks this run, each from its Michelin page opened tonight.

**San Ho Won, Mission, SF (2170 Bryant St) · Korean · Michelin One Star.** Korean cooking from chefs Corey Lee and Jeong-In Hwang; Michelin warns reservations are difficult to come by. +1 415-868-4479. https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/california/san-francisco/restaurant/san-ho-won

**The Progress, Fillmore, SF (1525 Fillmore St) · Californian · Michelin One Star.** A "NorCal-meets-Nordic" spot around the corner from the Fillmore, in two stories of wood, stone and tile. +1 415-673-1294. https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/california/san-francisco/restaurant/the-progress

**Flour + Water, Mission, SF (2401 Harrison St) · Italian · in the Michelin Guide.** An "always-packed Mission stalwart" built around pasta, with affable service in a buzzy, approachable room. +1 415-826-7000. https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/california/san-francisco/restaurant/flour-water

## Where to stay
Union Square has most major hotels and the best transit links. Civic Center/Hayes Valley puts you walking distance from Davies for Friday's symphony and Sunday's Head West market. For the Saturday market, Embarcadero hotels are steps from the Ferry Building; Fisherman's Wharf hotels are on the Sunday parade route, so expect street closures that morning. https://www.sftravel.com/neighborhoods
