## What's on (each confirmed on an official page)

**1) SF Symphony: Isabel Leonard Sings Ravel: Fri Nov 6, 7:30 pm** (also Sun Nov 8, 2 pm). Davies Symphony Hall, 201 Van Ness Ave. Fabien Gabel conducts Ravel's *Shéhérazade*, d'Indy's *Istar*, Debussy and Strauss. Free Inside Music Talk with Alicia Mastromonaco on stage one hour before each concert. Día de los Muertos altars in the lobby through Nov 11.
https://www.sfsymphony.org/Buy-Tickets/2026-27/Isabel-Leonard-Sings-Ravel

**2) SF Opera: Strauss In Concert: Fri Nov 6, 7:30 pm.** War Memorial Opera House, 301 Van Ness Ave. Music Director Eun Sun Kim leads the Opera Orchestra in *Till Eulenspiegel*, Strauss's *Four Last Songs* (soprano Kathleen O'Mara replacing Adela Zaharia), and a new *Der Rosenkavalier* suite arrangement in its U.S. debut. About 1 hour 33 minutes with one intermission; tickets from $30.
https://www.sfopera.com/seasons/fall-concert/

**3) ZooLights opens: Fri Nov 6 – Sun Nov 8 (and ongoing).** San Francisco Zoo, Sloat Blvd at the Great Highway. All-new holiday lights program through Jan 3, 2027 (Wed–Sun). Hours this weekend: Fri/Sat 5:30–10 pm, Sun 5:30–9 pm. Separate ticket from daytime Zoo admission; adults $30, children 2–11 $21; members $3 off. Reservations not required.
https://www.sfzoo.org/zoolights/

**4) Every Saturday Night (A.C.T. / SFBATCO): final performances through Sat Nov 7.** Strand Theater, 1127 Market St. Extended through Nov 7. A new musical moving between today's Fillmore and the 1950s "Harlem of the West." Recommended for ages 13+; showtimes and prices on the ticket page.
https://www.act-sf.org/whats-on/activate/every-saturday-night

**5) Ferry Plaza Farmers Market (Saturday): Sat Nov 7, 8 am–2 pm.** Ferry Building, Embarcadero and Market. The big weekly edition with 100+ vendors; free entry. Mostly sunny, near 70.
https://www.foodwise.org/farmers-markets/ferry-plaza-farmers-market/

**6) Warriors vs. Orlando Magic: Sat Nov 7, 5:30 pm PT.** Chase Center. Doors 4 pm. TV: NBCSBA/PCK-GSW; radio SiriusXM and 95.7 The Game. A Chase Center event ticket doubles as a Muni fare that day (not cable cars).
https://www.nba.com/warriors/game/0022600193-magic-vs-warriors-san-francisco-ca-11-07-2026

**7) Veterans Day Parade: Sun Nov 8, 11 am.** Fisherman's Wharf. Formation at 10 am on North Point (Powell to the Embarcadero); the route runs up the Embarcadero north of North Point and along Jefferson to Aquatic Park. Free to watch. F Market & Wharves switchbacks 8:30 am–3 pm (streetcars stay on Market / Embarcadero toward the Ferry Building); the 8, 28 and 39 also reroute.
https://www.sfmta.com/travel-updates/veterans-day-parade-sunday-november-8-2026

Also Sunday: Head West Marketplace in Hayes Valley, with Hayes, Linden and Octavia closed around Hayes and Octavia 6 am–7 pm (event hours aren't posted on the organizer's site). https://www.sfmta.com/travel-updates/head-west-marketplace-sunday-november-8-2026

## Where to stay
Union Square has most major hotels and the best transit links. Civic Center/Hayes Valley puts you walking distance from Davies and the Opera House for Friday's concerts and Sunday's Head West market. For the Saturday market, Embarcadero hotels are steps from the Ferry Building; Fisherman's Wharf hotels are on the Sunday parade route, so expect street closures that morning. For ZooLights, the Sunset / Parkmerced side is closest to the Zoo. https://www.sftravel.com/neighborhoods
