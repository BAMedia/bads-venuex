## Where to eat
No Yelp ratings (Yelp blocks automated loads). Rotated this run; descriptions from Michelin Guide indexed text and the restaurants' own sites.

**Zuni Café, Hayes Valley / Civic Center, SF (1658 Market St).** Own site: menus change daily, drawing on French, Italian and Mediterranean cooking with seasonal, organic ingredients; classics include house-cured anchovies, the Zuni Caesar and roast chicken for two. James Beard "Outstanding Restaurant" (2003). Dinner Tue–Sun 5–9:30 pm, lunch Fri–Sun 11 am–3 pm, closed Monday. Walkable to Davies for Sunday's and Tuesday's concerts. (415) 552-2522. https://zunicafe.com/

**Kin Khao, Union Square, SF (55 Cyril Magnin St, in Parc 55).** Michelin Guide One Star, Thai/Californian: Chef Pim Techamuanvivit's layered, produce-driven Thai cooking, such as hor mok curry mousse and a green curry with slow-cooked local rabbit; some dishes are very spicy. Own site hours: lunch daily 11:30 am–2 pm; dinner Sun–Thu 5:30–9 pm. (415) 362-7456. https://www.kinkhao.com/ · https://guide.michelin.com/ie/en/california/san-francisco/restaurant/kin-khao

**Tadich Grill, Financial District, SF (240 California St).** Own site: California's oldest continuously run restaurant (since 1849), known for mesquite-grilled seafood, casseroles and stews, and classic cocktails; reservations taken, walk-ins welcome, counter seats for one or two. Mon–Fri 11 am–9 pm, Sat 4–9 pm, **closed Sunday**. (415) 391-1849. https://www.tadichgrill.com/
