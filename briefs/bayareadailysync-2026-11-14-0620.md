## What's on (each confirmed on an official page)
Only four events held up on official pages this run. I dropped the Gautier Capuçon recital (the page I found was the 2025 date), and I did not list the SF Tea Festival because the organizer says Nov 14–15 is sold out.

**1) SF Symphony: Prokofiev's Romeo and Juliet, Sat Nov 14, 7:30 pm and Sun Nov 15, 2 pm.** Davies Symphony Hall, 201 Van Ness Ave. Marie Jacquot makes her Orchestral Series debut. Program: Holmès's Andromède, Respighi's Concerto gregoriano with violinist Sayaka Shoji, and selections from Prokofiev's Romeo and Juliet. Prices are on the ticket page (a partner listing shows $50–$185).
https://www.sfsymphony.org/Calendar/This-Month · programming release: http://www.sfsymphony.org/getmedia/9af39ed5-82f5-429c-9300-b3b79bcc303c/November-2026-SF-Symphony-programs.pdf

**2) Renegade Craft Fair, Winter Edition: Sat Nov 14 and Sun Nov 15, 11 am–5 pm.** Fort Mason Center Festival Pavilion, 2 Marina Blvd. 250+ curated artists. A third-party listing says entry is free with a suggested $5 contribution; the organizer page doesn't state that in what I read.
https://www.renegadecraft.com/event/san-francisco-winter/

**3) Fort Mason Farmers Market: Sun Nov 15, 9:30 am–1:30 pm.** Fort Mason Center. Free, rain or shine, 50+ farmers and food purveyors.
https://fortmason.org/event/sunday-farmers-market/2026-11-15/

**4) ZooLights: open Sat Nov 14 (5:30–10 pm) and Sun Nov 15 (5:30–9 pm); closed Mon and Tue.** San Francisco Zoo. Over one million lights across 14 zones. Adults $30, children 2–11 $21; members $3 off. Last ticket entry 9 pm Sat, 8 pm Sun. Sunday is forecast to be rainy.
https://www.sfzoo.org/zoolights/
