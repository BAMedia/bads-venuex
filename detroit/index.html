Getting around

QLINE: Free streetcar on Woodward, 3.3 miles from New Center to downtown. Monday–Saturday 8 am–12 am; Sunday 8 am–9 pm; arrivals about every 15 minutes. Thanksgiving hours were not listed on the page this run. https://www.qlinedetroit.com/

Detroit People Mover: Free. Today's service status: open and operating. Monday–Friday 6:30 am–12 am. Thanksgiving Day 6 am–9 pm. Weekday trains about every 7–15 minutes. Saturday hours were not listed on the schedule page this run. Huntington Place Station will be bypassed Nov 27–29 (facility closed). https://www.thepeoplemover.com/schedule

DDOT: The 4-Woodward is rerouted beginning 9 am Wednesday until 5 pm Thursday. Other downtown routes are detoured for the parade (6 am–5 pm Thursday) and the Turkey Trot (6–10 am Thursday). DDOT runs a Sunday schedule on Thanksgiving at regular fares. Temporary stop signs go up Wednesday. https://detroitmi.gov/departments/detroit-department-transportation/route-updates

Metered parking: Free on Thursday Nov 26 and Friday Nov 27 (meter violations only); safety violations still enforced. Normal enforcement resumes Saturday. (City of Detroit, Nov 23.)
